Insurance Desk Guide 2025
Owner: Member Services · Published 2025-09-15
Knowledge base: Insurance

## Rx pricing questions
When a member asks about prescription prices, confirm that their plan covers the drug, then transfer the call to the pharmacy team for a price estimate and the pricing disclaimer. Insurance advocates do not quote drug prices.

## Premium payment options
For: Insurance advocates and members
Members can pay their monthly premium by automatic bank draft, by card online, or by check. Automatic draft runs on the 3rd of each month.

## Claim status lookup
Look up the claim by member ID and date of service. Read the status and the expected decision date. Do not quote payment amounts before the claim is finalized.
