## Early refills
For: Pharmacy advocates and members
A refill can be processed once 75% of the days' supply has been used. Earlier requests are rejected as refill too soon; tell the member the first date they can refill.

## Vacation supply
For: Pharmacy advocates and members
Members travelling for more than 30 days can get a one-time vacation supply of their medication, up to 90 days, before they leave.
