Pharmacy Outreach Script 2024
Owner: Pharmacy Outreach · Published 2024-11-08

## Outreach introduction
Verbatim (Legal)
"Hello, I'm calling from your plan's pharmacy team about your prescriptions. This call is recorded."

## Refill reminder
Remind the member that their 30-day supply is due for refill and offer to switch to a 90-day mail-order supply.

## Rx pricing disclaimer
For: Pharmacy advocates and members
Verbatim (Legal)
"Prices quoted today are estimates based on your plan and are subject to change. Your final cost is confirmed when the pharmacy processes your prescription."

## Do-not-call request
If the member asks not to be called, confirm the request and add them to the do-not-call list before ending the call.
