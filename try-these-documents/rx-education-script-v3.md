## Recorded-line greeting
Verbatim (Legal)
"Thank you for calling. This call is recorded for quality and training purposes. May I have your full name and date of birth?"

## Mail-order 90-day supply
Maintenance drugs such as atorvastatin and metformin can be filled as a 90-day supply through mail-order pharmacy. Orders usually arrive in 5 to 7 business days with free standard shipping.

## Rx pricing disclaimer
Verbatim (Legal)
"The prices I share today are estimates based on your current plan and may change. Your final cost is confirmed when your prescription is processed."

## Confirm interest
Ask the member whether they want to move their prescriptions to mail order. Continue only on a clear, absolute yes. If the answer is anything else, thank them and go to the closing.

## Closing
Verbatim (Legal)
"Your decision today has no impact on your plan membership. Thank you for calling."
