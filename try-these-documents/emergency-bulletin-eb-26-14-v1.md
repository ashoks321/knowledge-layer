Emergency Bulletin EB-26-14 v1
Owner: Pharmacy Clinical Policy · Draft · last edited 2026-10-09
Applies to: Members in declared counties

## Early refills: member statement
For: Members
Verbatim (Legal)
"If you live in a county affected by Hurricane Delia, you can refill your prescriptions early, including insulin, until October 31, 2026. Use any network pharmacy, or call the number on your member ID card."

## Early refill override
For: Pharmacy advocates
For a member whose address is in a declared county, override the refill-too-soon rule with emergency code EM-14. Allow early refills of any covered drug, including insulin, up to a 30-day supply per fill, until October 31, 2026.

## Early refill calls to Insurance
For: Insurance advocates
If a member asks for an early refill because of the hurricane, check that their county is on the declared list and send the call to the pharmacy team. Insurance advocates cannot apply the override.

## Lost or damaged medication
For: Everyone
Members in a declared county who lost medication or had it damaged in the storm can get a replacement fill. No proof of loss is needed until October 31, 2026.

## Declared counties
For: Everyone
Applies to: Everyone
The emergency covers Bayshore, Cypress, Gulf, Mangrove, Palmetto and Pelican counties, effective October 9, 2026.
